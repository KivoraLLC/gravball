Grav Ball

You can't steer the ball. You can only change the world it falls through. Drag your finger to draw a gravity well, and the ball is pulled along the line you leave behind.

Fly as far as you can across seven handcrafted worlds — a neon skyline, a sunset coastline, a snowbound ridge, an erupting volcano, a glowing crystal shaft, and a coral reef where the sharks are bigger than you are. Or work through 36 hand-built puzzles where every hazard keeps moving while you plan.
