Drag to pull the ball

Physics arcade · iOS & Android

Grav Ball

You can't steer the ball. You can only change the world it falls through. Drag your finger to draw a gravity well, and the ball is pulled along the line you leave behind.

Fly as far as you can across seven worlds — a neon skyline, a sunset coastline, a snowbound ridge, an erupting volcano, a crystal shaft, a coral reef where the sharks are bigger than you are — or work through 36 puzzles where every hazard keeps moving while you plan.

Tap a row to slide it

Word puzzle · iOS & Android

Shiftword

Every row and every column has to spell a word. Slide a row sideways or a column up and down — letters wrap around the edge — until the whole grid reads in both directions.

Three grid sizes and a hundred levels each, three shared puzzles every day, and hidden second solutions to hunt down. Then there's Battle Shift: a ten-by-ten board, three minutes, and an opponent working the same letters.

The studio

Kivora

Kivora LLC is a one-person studio in Arkansas. Every game is built end to end here — code, art, sound and the puzzles themselves — which is why they stay small and why each one gets its own look.

Games are free, carry ads, and never ask for an account. Nothing you do in them leaves your phone.
